Three Spaces Where My Creativity Lives

My creativity tends to emerge in three places: on set, behind the decks, and around a table. Each space invites a different kind of storytelling—through visuals, sound, or food—but all share the same intention: bringing people together through thoughtful experiences.

Set

On set as a model and production assistant, I’ve learned to translate vision into execution. While modelling taught me how to live within a creative vision, production taught me how to build one. Working as a production assistant across culinary and design-led projects has shown me the often invisible labour behind seamless experiences. Supporting everything from load-in to final styling, I’ve learnt how timing, coordination, and attention to detail shape how a vision is ultimately realised.

Sound

As a DJ, I approach sound as a form of storytelling rooted in educating myself and others about the influence of Black Britons within UK music genres. The process of DJing—reading the environment, making real-time decisions, and shaping an experience through sound that feels cohesive from start to finish—mirrors production in curating a site of space-making that endures beyond the moment.

Table

The table is where food, music, and conversation intersect. I approach hosting like production—using prep-lists as scripts and ingredients as visuals to curate meaningful community experiences. It is the ultimate space for sensory storytelling.
