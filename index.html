Three Spaces Where My Creativity Lives

My creativity emerges in three places: on set, behind the decks, and around a table. Each space invites a different kind of storytelling—through visuals, sound, or food—but all share the same intention: bringing people together.

Set

While modeling taught me how to live within a creative vision, production taught me how to build one. My work as a production assistant in the culinary and design space has been a study in the 'invisible labor' of beauty. From the heavy lifting of load-ins to the delicate placement of a tablescape, I’ve learned that a truly immersive experience is 10% execution and 90% choreography. I don't just see a set; I see a living ecosystem where logistics meet aesthetics.

Sound

As a DJ, I think about sound as a narrative. I’m currently exploring the influence of Black Britons within UK music genres, using rhythm to bridge cultural gaps and move energy through a room.

Table

The table is where food, music, and conversation intersect. I approach hosting like production—curating the menu, atmosphere, and rhythm of the evening to create a meaningful community experience.
